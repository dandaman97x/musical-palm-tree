Integrated First Nations Unit

On February 08, 2008, after many years of relationship building initiatives, the Squamish Nation, Tsleil Waututh Nation, West Vancouver Police and the RCMP in North Vancouver & Squamish agreed to a new partnership creating the Integrated First Nations Unit (IFNU).
